# VetBridge Diagnostics --- Website & Digital Growth Prototype

## Project Brief

Build a polished, production-quality website prototype for **VetBridge
Diagnostics**, a veterinary diagnostics and animal-health company based
in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria.

This is not a generic veterinary clinic website. The goal is to create a
**digital front door for VetBridge**: a place where farmers, veterinary
professionals, clinics, pet owners, institutions and other potential
customers can quickly understand what VetBridge does, discover relevant
diagnostic services, understand the process, and contact the team.

The prototype is also a commercial demonstration. It will be presented
to the VetBridge team as a proposed digital presence and potential first
phase of a larger digital/AI customer-service system.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. CURRENT CUSTOMER JOURNEY

The existing VetBridge workflow is approximately:

1.  Customer makes a first call.
2.  Customer explains their complaint/problem.
3.  VetBridge doctors become involved.
4.  The doctor makes a preliminary prediction/assessment.
5.  Customer is told what sample is required for testing.
6.  Customer is informed about what the test is for and the current
    cost.
7.  Testing is performed.
8.  Results are sent online and explained.

The website should make this workflow easier to understand.

IMPORTANT: - Do not make the website pretend customers can diagnose
animals themselves. - Do not replace the veterinarian with the
website. - The website should help customers get to the right
professional/service faster. - Diagnostic explanations must be based on
VetBridge-approved information. - Do not invent medical claims, test
requirements, turnaround times or treatment recommendations.

# 6. DIAGNOSTIC SERVICES

This is one of the most important sections of the website.

Do NOT simply dump a technical price list on the page.

Turn the services into a clear, searchable/browsable catalogue.

Each service should communicate:

-   Service/test name
-   Plain-language explanation
-   Technical name where appropriate
-   What the test is for
-   Sample required, ONLY if confirmed by VetBridge
-   Current price, ONLY where confirmed
-   Relevant audience/category
-   CTA to enquire

Potential current services supplied by VetBridge:

-   Hygiene Audit --- ₦8,000
-   Microbial Analysis of Feed --- ₦8,000
-   Microbial Analysis for Water --- ₦8,000
-   Post Mortem Examination, Bacterial Isolation and Antibiotic
    Sensitivity Test --- ₦8,000
-   Vaccine Potency Test (ND Vaccine) --- ₦10,000
-   Seromonitoring for ND Antibody Titres --- ₦25,000
-   ELISA (IBV, IBDV, REO, NDV, MG, MS, ILT) BIOCHEK --- ₦25,000
-   IDVET AI: H9 NDV, NDV-S --- price not yet supplied
-   Day-Old Chick (DOC) Screening --- ₦58,000 / ₦43,000
-   Haemagglutination and Haemagglutination Inhibition Test --- ₦10,000

These prices are **tentative/current-for-now**, not guaranteed permanent
pricing.

Do not hard-code prices as permanent facts in a way that makes future
updates difficult.

Add an appropriate note such as:

> Prices shown are current indicative prices. Please contact VetBridge
> to confirm current pricing, availability and testing requirements.

Before finalizing the service catalogue, verify ambiguous items with
VetBridge.

In particular: - Clarify the two DOC Screening prices. - Confirm the
price/details of IDVET AI. - Confirm exact descriptions and sample
requirements. - Confirm which services are currently active/available.

# 17. BUILD PHILOSOPHY

This project should demonstrate:

**"I understand your business, not just your technology."**

Do not over-engineer.

Do not build features because they are technically interesting.

Every section should answer one of these:

-   Does this help someone understand VetBridge?
-   Does this help someone understand a service?
-   Does this reduce uncertainty?
-   Does this build trust?
-   Does this make contacting VetBridge easier?
-   Does this help VetBridge generate or qualify an enquiry?

If a feature does not contribute to one of those goals, leave it out.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. TECHNICAL DIRECTION

Use a modern frontend stack suitable for fast iteration.

Preferred: - Next.js - TypeScript - React - Modern CSS / Tailwind if
appropriate - Responsive design - Component-based architecture

Keep the code clean and easy to extend.

Potential future backend/API integration should not dictate the initial
design.

Build the initial prototype so that services, pricing, contact details
and content can later be moved into a CMS/database without requiring a
complete rewrite.
